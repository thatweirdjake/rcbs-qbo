RCBS Dashboard

RCBS Dashboard is an internal tool of Reality Check Business Solutions (RCBS). It reads accounting data (invoices and sales receipts) from RCBS's own QuickBooks Online company to build internal billing and labor-cost reports.

It is not offered, sold or distributed to third parties. Only authorized RCBS staff use it.

The application runs locally on RCBS workstations. To connect or reconnect QuickBooks, see Connect.
