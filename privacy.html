Privacy Policy

Who we are

RCBS Dashboard is an internal application operated by Reality Check Business Solutions ("RCBS") for its own use.

Data we access

With the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope, the App reads RCBS's own QuickBooks Online company data: company information, invoices and sales receipts (customer name, date, line items, quantities, amounts). The App only reads data; it does not create, change or delete records in QuickBooks.

How we use it

Solely to produce internal billing, revenue and labor-cost reports for RCBS management.

Storage and security

  • The App runs locally on RCBS workstations; data is not sent to any external server operated by RCBS or third parties.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored in a local credential file accessible only to authorized RCBS staff, and is never committed to code repositories or shared folders.
  • All communication with Intuit uses HTTPS/TLS.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share QuickBooks data with any third party. Reports stay within RCBS.

Retention

Report files are kept only as long as needed for internal reporting. Disconnecting the App and deleting the local credential file ends all access.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps → Disconnect).
